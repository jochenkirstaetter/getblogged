After my initial participation as learner in the ALC 3.1 programme and as in last year's ALC 4.0 programme (aka GADS 2019) I decided to give back more this year.

Back in February I submitted again to be a mentor in this year's initiative but also applied for the position of a voluntary Programme Assistant (PA). Surprisingly both applications were successful.

## What is so interesting about GADS?

Similar to last year the programme offers around 33,000 applicants access to high quality learning material over several months for free. Additionally, learners are connected with experienced mentors in various Slack channels. The job of the mentors is to answer learner questions, to help them overcome technical issues, to unblock them with some guidance, and to monitor their progress with regular exchange.

Given the three different specialisations in regards to Google technology - Android, Mobile Web and Cloud Platform - the ratio of mentors to mentees varies between 1:11 (better said 2:22 in one team) and 1:42.

## The various phases of GADS 2020

Like previous iterations the programme has multiple phases which kind of act like a funnel to sieve and select learners based on their engagement in each phase. And let's be clear: it's not only about watching video courses provided by Pluralsight.
