Amazon feedback  
I really appreciate this collection of hands on strategies to run a start-up or an existing business. There are a good number of issues I've experienced myself over the past years, and there are some very author-related, biased opinions on certain topics.

Overall it is a quick read and reflection on own activities. Not sure whether it is a convenient title for beginners.

The rating is influenced by the poor quality of language in large areas of the book. An editorial process would surely improve the overall quality.

I hope Ashvin is going to provide a second edition, and I wish him all the best for the next book, as mentioned several times in this title.
